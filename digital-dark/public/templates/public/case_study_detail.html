Executive Summary

ActiveGear Australia was spending $12,000/month on Google Ads with an agency reporting a "3.2x ROAS", but their net profit margin was stagnating due to broad non-converting search terms and Liquid theme canonical issues split-ranking their collection pages. Digital Darts audited the account, restructured Performance Max by SKU profit margins, and executed a technical Liquid SEO overhaul.

The Challenge

When ActiveGear reached out to Digital Darts, their e-commerce store faced three major bottlenecks:

  • 1. Vanity ROAS Metrics: Ad campaigns targeted broad non-converting search queries like "men's gear" which reported clicks but converted low-margin items.
  • 2. Liquid Canonical Duplication: Shopify linked products via `/collections/product/`, diluting page authority across Google's search index.
  • 3. High Cart Abandonment: Checkout page lacked trust badges and smooth slide-out cart upsells.

The Strategy & Execution

Digital Darts deployed our 4-Step Profit Framework over a 6-month period:

Step 1: Profit Margin & Break-Even ROAS Calculation

We audited product gross margins, shipping costs, and Shopify transaction fees to set exact net profit targets for every campaign.

Step 2: Performance Max SKU Margin Clustering

We separated top-tier margin products into high-priority PMax asset groups while adding 300+ negative brand keywords to force clean attribution.

Step 3: Technical Liquid SEO & Collection Siloing

We edited `main-product.liquid` to force canonical URLs to root `/products/` and built editorial backlinks targeting active search phrases.
